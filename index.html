How Empirical Shannon Lexical Entropy is Computed on Output Data

In information theory, Empirical Shannon Entropy H quantifies the unpredictability, vocabulary richness, and information density of the text generated by the model:

1. Empirical Shannon Lexical Entropy: Calculated directly from the observed word probability distribution in the model's generated output:
H(text) = −∑ p(wi) × log2 p(wi)  (bits / word)
2. Monotonic Scaling Across Presets: As Temperature and Top-K increase from most-predictable (T: 0.0, K: 1) to most-creative (T: 1.2, K: 100), the token probability distribution flattens, resulting in higher observed entropy (more diverse vocabulary and metaphors).
